Our Mission

Empowering Bangladesh's Entrepreneurs with Next-Gen Cash Accounting

SoftobroCashBook was conceived to address a critical gap in the Bangladesh SME market: reliable, real-time daily cashbook management with enterprise-grade data isolation.

Small and medium business owners often deal with manual paper khata books, risky shared spreadsheets, or accounting tools too convoluted for quick cash-in and cash-out recording. SoftobroCashBook pairs the speed and simplicity of CashBook with genuine multi-tenant database isolation.

Our Architectural Pillars
  • Dedicated Tenant Database:

    Every verified business owner gets an independent PostgreSQL database. Your records are never stored in a shared table.

  • Bangladeshi Financial Flow:

    Complete native support for BDT (৳) currency, bKash payments, and print layouts structured for Bangladeshi business conventions.

  • Reliable Hosting & APIs:

    Engineered for seamless deployment on cPanel hosting with Python Passenger, backed by Django REST Framework APIs for upcoming mobile apps.
