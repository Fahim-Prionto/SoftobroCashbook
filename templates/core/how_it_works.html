Workflow

How SoftobroCashBook Operates

From registration to your private tenant database in 3 straightforward steps.

Step 1

Register Account & Select Plan

Create your account with your business name, contact phone, and email. Choose a subscription package (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly) tailored to the number of businesses and cashbooks you require.

Your account is initially created in a pending verification status.

Simple Registration

No credit cards required. Only your business name and contact number.

Step 2

Make Payment via bKash

Open your bKash mobile app or dial *247# to transfer the exact subscription amount in BDT to our official account. Submit your 8-10 character bKash Transaction ID (TrxID) and an optional payment screenshot.

bKash Verified Platform

Quick, secure, and familiar payment workflow built for Bangladesh.

Step 3

Verification & Database Provisioning

Once our Super Admin confirms your payment, the system dynamically provisions a dedicated PostgreSQL database specifically for your business, runs tenant migrations, and activates your Service Admin portal.

Dedicated Database Isolation

High-performance private ledger with zero risk of cross-tenant data leaks.
